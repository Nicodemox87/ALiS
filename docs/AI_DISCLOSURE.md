# AI-assisted development disclosure

ALiS was developed with assistance from generative AI tools (OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex), including support for source code, debugging, tests, documentation and visual assets. Scientific direction, requirements and release decisions remain with Dr Nicodemo Abate, Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche — Istituto di Scienze del Patrimonio Culturale (CNR-ISPC). AI is not an author or a certifying authority.

This is a transparent statement of development provenance, not an assertion that every component was generated by AI or reviewed line by line by a human. Automated regression tests are documented separately and do not establish scientific accuracy or replace expert review. Users must validate classifications, models and derived scientific products.

AI-assisted development is distinct from the ML/DL classification algorithms exposed by ALiS. No ChatGPT connection is required to process a cloud. The update checker sends no clouds, models or project content to GitHub; see [update behaviour](UPDATES.md).

The statement does not alter the GPL-2.0-or-later software licence, third-party rights, or applicable documentation/asset licences. It does not imply institutional legal approval, confer rights over third-party material, or certify regulatory conformity.

## Regulatory context (checked 21 October 2026)

This disclosure is adopted as a project transparency measure. It is **not** described as a universal statutory label required on all AI-assisted software. Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 addresses specified AI interactions and synthetic content, with conditions and exceptions. The Commission's current guidance distinguishes source code from synthetic content subject to marking and explains the conditions for public-interest text and substantive human review/editorial control. Applicability must be assessed for each actual use; this notice is not legal advice.

Italian Law 23 September 2025, no. 132, Article 25, amends copyright provisions concerning human intellectual work created with AI assistance. It does not make an AI system an author or make this notice a substitute for assessing authorship and rights.
